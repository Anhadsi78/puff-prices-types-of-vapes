Disposable vapes

A disposable vape is sold already filled with e-liquid and is not designed to be refilled. Some models have a sealed battery that cannot be charged; others include a charging port so the battery can be topped up. Either way, once the e-liquid runs out the whole unit is discarded and replaced. Browse current disposable listings on the site.

Pod systems

A pod system is a reusable device with a removable e-liquid reservoir called a pod. Some pods arrive already filled and are discarded after use; others arrive empty and are refilled from a separate bottle. "Open system" and "closed system" are industry terms, not definitions in Canadian law: an open-system device accepts e-liquid or pods a buyer chooses, and a closed-system device is designed to work only with the manufacturer's own prefilled pods or cartridges. See pod system listings.

Hardware and e-liquid

Vape hardware covers reusable devices and parts sold apart from any e-liquid, such as mods, tanks, batteries, coils and replacement parts. E-liquid is the liquid used in a refillable device, sold by the bottle. It is not always a nicotine liquid; nicotine-free e-liquid is also sold. Where nicotine is present, Canada caps the concentration. Bottles may be labelled as nicotine salt or freebase, which are trade terms for different chemical forms of the same nicotine.
